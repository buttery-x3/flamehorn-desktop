## What changed and what is usable

This is the first runnable desktop candidate. It includes offline Workspaces, Context/Reference/Process graphs, full inspector editing, semantic zoom, all thirteen Processes, explicit request previews, retained versions, Stop and deliberate recovery, managed text/image/PDF files, historical extraction, Undo/Trash/purge, library archives and daily backups.

Both provider paths are implemented. Authorised live text checks passed with ChatGPT `gpt-6-luna` and local Gemma through Responses and Chat Completions. Multi-output failure/recovery is verified with a controlled HTTP fixture; it is not evidence of real-model output quality. Unknown modality and context capabilities remain visible and can block unsupported requests.

Final review fixed clipped Run controls at smaller zoom, closing during browser sign-in, protected-save failures after credential rotation, and accidental inclusion of older build output in the package.

## Known limits and remaining gates

- This is a usable implementation preview, **not the accepted complete MVP**. Live token expiry/rotation, remote revocation and image/PDF inference remain unverified; their controlled behavior and local capability blocking are tested.
- Local complete-context preservation at server limits is not verified. The app warns about unknown overflow behavior and blocks profiles declared to truncate.
- A second pristine Windows machine, other DPI settings and large-library performance are not tested. No historical released schema exists to migrate; unknown schemas are refused safely.
- Playwright sometimes fails to attach to its loopback debugger during launch. Retried instrumented runs passed; ordinary executable launch passed separately. This test-harness problem remains undiagnosed.
- PDFs have no OCR. Text-only extraction can omit layout/diagrams; acknowledgement is required. Imports are capped at 25 MiB/100 PDF pages; portable archives at 512 MiB expanded data.
- Backups/exports can retain removed material. Purge protects surviving history and is not secure erasure. Archives contain private library material and are not encrypted.
